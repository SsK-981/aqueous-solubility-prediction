# Third-party attribution

Model adaptations in `models/` draw on the
[PNNL solubility prediction implementation](https://github.com/pnnl/solubility-prediction-paper)
and [Panapitiya et al., ACS Omega](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.2c00642).
The original Battelle notice is reproduced below and applies to its upstream material.
This snapshot makes no additional license grant for original thesis code.

Data sources include [AqSolDBc](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03105-6),
[SOMAS](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01814-4), and
[BigSolDB](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-05559-8). Obtain data from their
respective sources and check the version and conditions for your use.

The ESOL baseline uses the RDKit-refitted equation published by
[Pat Walters](https://github.com/PatWalters/solubility), based on
[Delaney's ESOL model](https://doi.org/10.1021/ci034243x). Obtain the
DLS-100 validation subset from the upstream repository.
